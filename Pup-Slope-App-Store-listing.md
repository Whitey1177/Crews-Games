## Description
Grab your board and shred the mountain with Crew and friends!

Pup Slope is a relaxing-but-addictive endless snowboarding game starring eight real dogs — each with their own power, signature flip trick and theme song.

PICK YOUR PUP
• Crew – double jump
• Dixie – long speed boosts
• Winnie – faster flips
• Penny – higher jumps
• Rufus – grind master
• Scout – no powers, hard mode
• Gracie – tough landings
• Casey – glides through the air

SHRED THE SLOPE
• One-tap controls: tap to jump, hold to flip
• Land flips for a speed boost that smashes rocks
• Grind rails, chase squirrels and collect bones
• Watch out — after 2,500 m the dog catcher is on your tail!

MAKE IT YOURS
• Spend bones on hats, boards and scarves
• Complete missions to level up
• Day turns to night as you ride further

COMPETE
• Every dog has its own global leaderboard — prove you're the best Crew, Dixie or Casey rider out there

Free to play. Contains ads.

## Review notes (App Review Information → Notes)
Pup Slope needs no login. Leaderboards: after a run, type any nickname on the wipeout screen and tap Post. Names are filtered for profanity, any player can report a name with the ⚑ button, and the developer removes reported names. The rewarded "Keep riding" ad appears after a wipeout (once per run).
