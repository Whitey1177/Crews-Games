3. Global leaderboard

Posting to the leaderboard is optional. When you tap Post, we store the nickname you type, your score or distance, the game and the time it was posted. Your nickname and score are publicly visible to everyone who plays. Please use a nickname, not your real name or anything that identifies you.

We keep each game's top 100 entries; entries that drop out of the top 100 are deleted automatically. You can ask us to remove your entry at any time (see Your rights). Offensive nicknames may be removed.

4. Advertising

To keep the Games free we may show ads from third-party ad networks (for example Google AdSense / AdMob). These partners may use cookies, similar technologies or device identifiers to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure performance and prevent fraud.

  • Personalized ads (based on your interests) are shown only if you give consent. You can withdraw consent any time from Privacy choices and you will still be able to play.
  • If you don't consent, or if you tell us you are under 16, you'll see non-personalized ads based on context (for example the page you're on) rather than your behavior.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, consent for ads is collected through a consent tool that follows the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework, where required by our ad partners.

Learn how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. Cookies and device storage

The Games themselves only use your browser's local storage to save your progress, settings and consent choices. This is necessary for the game to work and stays on your device; we can't see it. You can delete it any time with Privacy choices → Delete my game data on this device, or by clearing your browser's website data. Ad partners may set their own cookies as described in section 4.

6. Who we share data with

  • Netlify, Inc. (hosting and leaderboard storage): netlify.com/privacy
  • Ad partners (when ads are enabled), such as Google: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Authorities, if required by law.

Some of these providers are located in the United States. Where data is transferred outside the EEA/UK, it is protected by appropriate safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep data

  • Device data: until you delete it.
  • Leaderboard entries: while they're in a game's top 100, or until you ask us to remove them.
  • Hosting logs: according to Netlify's retention periods (typically short-term).
  • Feedback: as long as needed to handle it, then deleted.

8. Your rights

If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland (and in many other places), you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy (portability);
  • have it corrected or deleted;
  • restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time (this doesn't affect processing before withdrawal) via Privacy choices;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To use these rights, email [your contact email]. For leaderboard removal, include the game and nickname. Because we don't use accounts, we may ask you to confirm details (for example the score and roughly when it was posted) so we remove the right entry. We will reply within one month.

US privacy rights

Depending on your state, you may have rights to know, delete and correct personal information and to opt out of "sale" or "sharing" for targeted advertising. Choosing Only necessary in Privacy choices opts you out of personalized (targeted) ads. We honor Global Privacy Control signals where required.

9. Children

The Games are suitable for a general audience. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you tell us you are under 16, we only allow non-personalized ads. Children should use a made-up nickname on the leaderboard and ask a parent before posting. Parents can contact us to remove anything their child posted.

10. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with our hosting provider. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we limit what we collect so there is very little to protect.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as the Games change (for example when ads are switched on). We'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, ask for your choices again in the game.
