3. Leaderboards

Each dog has its own leaderboard. Posting is optional. When you tap Post, we store the nickname you type, your distance, how long you rode, the dog you played and the time it was posted. Nicknames and distances are publicly visible. Please use a nickname, not your real name or anything that identifies you.

Offensive names are blocked by a filter, can be reported by any player with the ⚑ button, and are removed by us. We keep each board's top 100 entries; others are deleted automatically. You can ask us to remove your entry at any time (see Your rights).

4. Advertising

The iPhone app shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional video you can choose to watch to keep riding after a wipeout, and an occasional full-screen ad between runs. The website does not show ads.

  • Personalized ads (based on your interests) are shown only with your consent. On iPhone, Apple asks whether Pup Slope may track you; if you say no, Google cannot use your device's advertising identifier.
  • If you don't consent, or tell us you are under 16, you'll see non-personalized ads based on context rather than your behavior.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, consent for ads is collected through Google's consent message, which follows the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework.
  • You can change your choices any time from 🔒 Privacy choices in the Game, and in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. Device storage and cookies

The Game uses your device's local storage to save your progress, settings and privacy choices. This is necessary for the Game to work and stays on your device; we can't see it. You can delete it any time with Privacy choices → Delete my game data on this device, by clearing your browser's website data, or by deleting the app. Google may use its own identifiers in the app as described in section 4.

6. Who we share data with

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (hosting and leaderboard storage): cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • Google LLC (AdMob ads in the iPhone app): policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple distributes the iPhone app; Apple's own privacy policy applies to the App Store.
  • Authorities, if required by law.

These providers may process data in the United States. Where data is transferred outside the EEA/UK, it is protected by appropriate safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep data

  • Device data: until you delete it or the app.
  • Leaderboard entries: while they're in a board's top 100, or until you ask us to remove them.
  • Name reports: until we review them.
  • Spam-limit records: about 10 minutes.
  • Hosting and ad data: according to Cloudflare's and Google's retention periods.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EEA, UK, Switzerland and many US states), you have the right to access or get a copy of your personal data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent at any time, opt out of targeted advertising ("sale" or "sharing"), and complain to your data protection authority. Choosing Only necessary in Privacy choices, or not allowing tracking on iPhone, opts you out of personalized ads.

To use these rights, email [contact email]. For leaderboard removal, include the dog and nickname. Because we don't use accounts, we may ask for details (like the distance and roughly when it was posted) to find the right entry. We reply within one month.

9. Children

The Game is suitable for a general audience but is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you tell us you are under 16, we only allow non-personalized ads. Children should ask a parent before posting a nickname. Parents can contact us to remove anything their child posted.

10. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We keep very little data, so there is very little to protect, but no system is 100% secure.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and, for important changes, ask for your choices again in the Game.
