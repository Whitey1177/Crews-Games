Common questions

I lost my bones / items. Progress is saved on your device. It's lost if you delete the app or clear website data, and it doesn't move between devices.

How do I post to the leaderboard? After a run, type a nickname on the wipeout screen and tap Post. Each dog has its own leaderboard; you can only post to the dog you played.

Someone has an offensive name. Tap ⚑ next to the name on the leaderboard, or email us with the dog and the name.

How do I stop personalized ads? Tap 🔒 Privacy choices on the start screen and choose "Only necessary". On iPhone you can also turn off tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The leaderboard won't load. It needs an internet connection. The game itself works offline.
